    Your new desktop awaits. 🚀

    The open-source HTML dynamic desktop engine for Windows 10 & 11.

    Download GitHub

    Quick and easy

    Customizing your desktop has never been easier. Create your own live, interactive wallpapers with HTML, CSS, and JS, or browse and download others' creations right from the app.

    Open-source

    Octos is driven by its community. Contribute your own wallpaper or check out the repository on GitHub. And, of course, Octos and all wallpapers are completely free. Join our community!

    Performant

    Octos is optimized for any and all web technologies to run smoothly on the desktop, like by pausing when an app is in fullscreen. Octos is designed for all machines, from beefed-up PCs to battery-powered laptops.

    Powerful

    Octos provides loads of powerful and easy-to-use features, from music visualization and media controls right on your desktop to multi-monitor synchronization. Try it out for yourself!
